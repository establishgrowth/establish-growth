Questions

Good to know

How fast will I get my content?

Event sneak-peek reels arrive within 72 hours and full recaps within 7 days. Brand Story and Marketing projects usually take 1–2 weeks after the shoot, depending on scope. Rush delivery is available.

How does payment work?

A 50% deposit books your date. The remaining 50% is due when your final files are delivered.

What if I want changes?

Every project includes 2 rounds of revisions. Extra changes beyond that are billed at $85 per hour.

Do you travel?

We're based in the Twin Cities and cover Minneapolis–St. Paul and surrounding areas. Travel beyond 30 miles is billed by mileage.
