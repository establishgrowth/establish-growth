    Questions

    Good to know

    Is it really free?

    Yes. No card, no catch. You'll get real, useful next steps whether or not you ever work with us.

    How does it work?

    You pick what to check. We load your website on a phone and a desktop the way Google and your visitors do, using Google's own testing tools. Your quick answers cover the parts a scan can't see, like your story and reviews.

    Why is my phone score lower than I expected?

    Phones are tested on a typical mobile connection, which is slower than your office Wi-Fi. That's what most of your visitors experience. Scores can vary a little between runs.

    The check couldn't read my website. Why?

    Some websites block automated tools. You'll still get your branding and marketing results, and Elliot will review your website by hand.

    What's the difference from the Full Strategy Report?

    The Brand Health Check shows where your brand stands today. The Full Strategy Report looks deeper into your brand, your competition and your systems, with a real-customer test of how you reply and sell.
